# TradePulse AI — Narrated hackathon demo

This is the speaking outline for the **170-second hybrid screenshot and motion-graphics demo**. It complements, rather than replaces, a live demonstration of the `/demo` endpoint. The narration is illustrative and should not imply live USSD, production deployment, real money movement, or measured sales improvement.

**Scene 01:** Picture a sales route with ten retailers: two are buying, eight are quiet. Sales data tells us what happened, not why.

**Scene 02:** Is it shelf space, working capital, or deliveries? TradePulse begins by asking the retailer, not guessing.

**Scene 03:** A numbered-menu conversation captures a retailer-stated barrier in the browser-simulated feature-phone flow. This is not connected to a live telecom network.

**Scene 04:** Each observation is saved with a trace. A retailer statement is a signal, not a verified order or demand.

**Scene 05:** Meta-powered analysis interprets the evidence and presents an advisory summary with uncertainty, constraints, and risks. It does not make commercial decisions.

**Scene 06:** A manager reviews the suggestion and may accept it for planning. AI cannot authorize rewards or payments.

**Scene 07:** The manager drafts a small incentive mission with conditions, a minimum order and financial limits.

**Scene 08:** Drafting is not approval: separate human activation is required. This is only a synthetic demonstration.

**Scene 09:** A fictional retailer commits, and the prototype records an explicitly simulated purchase. No real sale is verified.

**Scene 10:** The model calculates one thousand naira in simulated revenue, six hundred in product cost, fifty in fulfillment and ten in operating costs.

**Scene 11:** An eligible mission may receive a fifty-naira simulated reward only after explicit human authorization. No money is transferred.

**Scene 12:** The scenario leaves two hundred and ninety naira in contribution after the reward; the unchanged baseline is shown separately. This is not measured uplift.

**Scene 13:** TradePulse AI. Listen, understand, and plan with evidence. An experiment in better retail activation, not a claim of real-world success.

## Publishing checklist

- Record the functional browser demonstration separately when possible.
- Prefer a clear human or natural-quality narration; local synthesized speech is an optional fallback.
- Keep visible **synthetic** and **simulated** disclaimers in every relevant financial scene.
- Host the MP4 at the URL required by the hackathon submission form and test the link in a signed-out browser.
- Put the public GitHub repository URL and video link in the final entry.
